      READING THE SCREEN

      GREEN ARCS
      where your guns can reach — bright means loaded and bearing
      RED BRACKET
      an enemy. GUNS ON YOU means her broadside is lined up
      RED ARROWS
      enemies off the edge of the view, with the range to each
      DASHED RING
      a hostile shore battery's reach — do not loiter inside it

      WINNING

      Hold more of the eight Holds than the enemy and their Colours bleed away. Strike them to nothing, or simply be ahead at the bell. A battery must be silenced before anyone can land.
